## Audience

- **Primary:** Shopify merchants and ecommerce operators (solo founders to mid-market brands) who sell on Shopify and worry about declining organic visibility.
- **Secondary:** Shopify agencies and CRO consultants managing multiple storefronts.
- **Expertise level:** Intermediate. Readers know SEO basics but are new to AEO, GEO, and llms.txt.
- **Problems they solve:**
  - Products not appearing in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews recommendations
  - Unclear which AI crawlers are allowed on their storefront
  - Missing structured data and machine-readable catalog context
  - No way to measure AI referral traffic or competitor citation share
- **Misconceptions:**
  - "Traditional SEO is enough for AI search"
  - "Blocking all AI bots protects my store"
  - "AI shopping is still too small to matter"

## Positioning

- **Mission:** Help Shopify merchants become discoverable and citable in AI-powered search and shopping experiences.
- **Point of view:** AEO is not a replacement for SEO. It is a parallel optimization layer for how LLMs read, cite, and recommend your catalog. Merchants who ignore it are invisible to the fastest-growing acquisition channel on Shopify.
- **What we are NOT:** A generic SEO agency blog, a hype-driven "AI will replace Google" publication, or a tool that only scores without actionable fixes.
- **Competitors:**
  - Manual SEO audits: slow, no AI-citation tracking, no llms.txt deployment
  - Generic SEO apps: keyword-focused, no ChatGPT/Perplexity visibility scoring
  - DIY schema plugins: partial coverage, no integrated AI rewrite workflow

- Fabricate statistics or case studies

- Over-promise "rank #1 in ChatGPT"
