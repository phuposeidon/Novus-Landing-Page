## What “AEO Audit” Means on Shopify

An AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) audit checks whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI, and similar assistants can read, trust, and cite your store — not only whether Google ranks your pages.

A useful Shopify AEO audit usually covers:

- **Crawl / bot access:** `robots.txt`, AI crawler allowances, and Cloudflare or CDN firewall blocks.
- **Structure:** JSON-LD schema, headings hierarchy, FAQ blocks, and policy documentation.
- **Catalog readiness:** Thin product descriptions, missing image alt text, and incomplete variant attributes.
- **AI discovery files:** `llms.txt` and machine-readable agent context.
- **Proof:** Whether your brand actually appears in AI answers for high-intent buyer prompts.

## How to Run an AEO Audit on Your Shopify Store (5 Steps)

Follow this five-step workflow to audit and improve your store's AI search footprint:

1. **Confirm AI crawler access:** Verify that `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, and `Google-Extended` are not blocked in your `robots.txt.liquid` or by CDN firewall rules. See our [Shopify AI bot manager guide](/blog/ai-bot-manager-shopify-robots-txt/) for details.
2. **Run a full-store AEO/SEO audit:** Scan all products, collections, content pages, and policies for missing structured data and thin copy.
3. **Fix high-impact gaps first:** Deploy valid [JSON-LD schema](/blog/shopify-json-ld-schema-ai-search/), publish an [llms.txt file](/blog/llms-txt-shopify-stores/), and clarify the first two sentences of top-selling product descriptions.
4. **Track 5–20 buyer prompts:** Set up an [AI Rank Tracker](/blog/ai-rank-tracker-shopify-setup/) to monitor whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity recommend your brand for key category queries.
5. **Re-audit weekly:** As products, prices, and collections change, schedule recurring scans to catch new crawler blocks or schema regressions.
