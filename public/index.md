# Nexis CRO — AI SEO & AEO Audit for Shopify

Your store is invisible to AI search engines.

Nexis CRO audits your Shopify store for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, and Bing Copilot —
then fixes what's broken with one-click AI.

## Product

- **AEO audits** — crawl products, collections, pages, and policies; score AEO readiness
- **AI rewrites** — one-click fixes for issues surfaced in audits
- **AI Rank Tracker** — track citation share of voice vs competitors across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity
- **Schema blocks & llms.txt** — inject JSON-LD and deploy AI context files
- **Bulk alt text & query lab** — AI-powered catalog and prompt tooling
